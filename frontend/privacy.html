Privacy and accountability

Students should be able to raise concerns safely. This notice explains how information is handled in this prototype.

What we collect

  • The title and description you write, your campus and location, urgency, whether you flagged a safety risk, and how many students you say are affected.
  • Your visibility choice (public or private), a timestamp, a reference number, and a one-way hash of your access code (never the code itself).

We do not ask for your name, student number, email or phone number, and you do not need an account.

The limits of anonymity

Submitting without an account does not make you impossible to identify. Details in your text, or the circumstances of the concern, can reveal who you are to people who know the situation. Like any website, the web server may record network information such as IP addresses in its logs. Remove names and ID numbers you do not want reviewers to see.

Your access code

Your reference identifies your concern; your access code proves it is yours. The code is shown once and stored only as a one-way hash, so nobody, including the Guild and system administrators, can read, resend or recover it. Repeated wrong codes cause a temporary wait. Never share it: leaders will never ask for it.

What is public

If you choose “Public”, the public list shows the title and description, campus, category, status and official public replies. Private replies, internal staff notes and your access code are never public. Concerns in sensitive categories, such as harassment or discrimination, are always private whatever you choose.

Who can see private concerns

  • You, using your access code.
  • The Guild President, who reviews and assigns concerns.
  • The delegate a concern is assigned to.

System administrators manage staff accounts and cannot open complaints. Every staff view and action on a concern is recorded in an audit log.

How long information is kept

Retention has not yet been decided. The Guild and the university still need to agree how long resolved concerns are kept before deletion or anonymisation, and this notice will be updated when they do.

About this platform

Saagi SyncCampus is a final year project prototype. Before any real use it should be reviewed under ISBAT University’s data protection policy and the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.
