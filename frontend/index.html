How it works

Four simple steps from problem to confirmed solution.

1
SubmitChoose a category, say what happened and where, and how many students are affected.
2
Prioritised and assignedThe system scores urgency, links repeated reports and recommends the right leader. The President confirms.
3
Followed upThe leader updates you. If time runs out, reminders and escalation happen automatically.
4
You confirmA concern is closed only after you say it is resolved. If not, it is reopened.

Know before you submit

Short version of the privacy and accountability notice.

Your name is not askedDo not type names, faces or ID numbers you want kept private. Details can still identify you.
Your access code is yours aloneShown once and cannot be recovered. Guild leaders will never ask you for it.
Sensitive concerns stay privateHarassment and discrimination reports are always private, whatever you choose.
Every staff action is loggedWho viewed or changed a concern is recorded for accountability.
